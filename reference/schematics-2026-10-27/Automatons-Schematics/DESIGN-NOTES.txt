CONSTRUCTION DETAILS RESOLVED FOR THE SCHEMATICS

These notes specify small choices necessary to turn the approved layouts into
actual blocks. They do not change the gameplay specification.

All six new worksites contain their actual custom totem block at the agreed
anchor, with no copied owner, network, job, charge, inventory, or saved identity.
The custom block mapping follows resource names present in the supplied Codex
history. Codex must confirm it against the actual repository before release.

CRAFTERTON
7x7 cobblestone floor, oak plank walls and log corners, peaked spruce stair/slab
roof, two side windows, spruce door and entrance lantern. The totem is D2, not
D1. Stations are B2/B3/B4, storage F3-F4 on two levels, empty armor stand B6.
The stand is a real entity with no armor; the Crafterton fills it later.

HUNTERTON
7x7 floor, plain oak walls, flat spruce roof, door D7, totem D1. Two stacked
double chests at B2-B3 face into the room. Side windows and a hanging entrance
lantern provide a simple finished shed without extra furniture.

RANCHERTON
The landscape-oriented 7x9 shed is A5:G13, attached to G1:W17 fenced pasture.
Its east face is open above the fence line. Gate G9 faces east. Side door D13
faces south. Two single-level double chests, two hay bales, an empty milk tank,
totem B9, and an item frame with supplied shears match the coordinates. The
roof is a spruce gable over the nine-block width. The pen's grass is terrain
to adopt or prepare, not automatically supplied livestock or instant grazing.

QUARRYTON
7x7 shed E1:K7 with flat roof, eight double chests, three-wide central aisle.
Door H7, path H8/H9, shaft mouth H10. Four starter ladder rungs are below ground
with backing on the H9 side. There is no unsupported rung in the open mouth.
The 15x15 quarry at A11:O25 is entirely PRESERVE at build time. It is metadata
for the Quarryton's incremental excavation, not a pre-excavated hole. Workers
extend the shaft and lighting as they descend.

LUMINARITON
Tower body is seven by seven. The rear curved stone supports leave the lower
front open with three blocks of clearance, enough for a 2.5-block Guardington.
The totem is ground-level D1. The workshop walking surface is at +4; D2 remains
an open ladder hatch. C2 provides real backing through the hatch, with an upper
exit at E2. This uses one otherwise vacant upper C2 cell for ladder support.
The workshop has two tanks, an Infusing Pedestal, two stacked double chests,
windows, purple wall banners, and a five-tier dark-oak roof. Its one-block roof
overhang is why the export includes an extra northern row.

The 25x25 courtyard has twelve 4x4 beds, three-wide paths and the 11x11 central
clearing. Beds contain grass/soil and Collectors, no free flowers. There are
exactly 180 runtime planting cells. No trees or saplings are included.

Rotation needs special attention: the existing Collector scan is world-aligned
at offsets -1..+2. Rotate the bed bounds, then place its Collector at minimum
world X+1 and minimum world Z+1. Recompute the other fifteen planting cells.
Blindly rotating the off-center Collector position will shift its scan footprint.

FISHERTON
7x7 hut C1:I7 with a modest spruce gable, door F7, totem F1 and two stacked
double chests B2-B3 in hut-local coordinates. Dock E8:G15 is exactly 3x8.
All six bay reservations and the center walkway are in the manifest. No chairs
are physical blocks or entities in these files; they remain a worker visual.

The 10x7 source rectangle C8:I17 is a validation mask beneath/around the dock,
not placed water. Dock floor blocks occupy logical Y=-1 and required sources
occupy Y=-2, leaving intact water under the deck. Their surface is one block
below the walking surface. This is a minimum site check and does not waive
vanilla fishing loot/open-water requirements. The diagram shows the water only
to make the site relationship understandable.

FARMINGTON
9x9 unplanted field with one central source at E5, dirt beneath that source,
and a full glass block above it. Three strips contain 27, 26 and 27 crop cells.
Use real water and planting stock; prepare/adopt soil and till with a hoe.

MININGTON
The strip-level file preserves surrounding stone instead of substituting a
generic rock type. It contains explicit passage air, torches, the cobblestone
depot floor, four double chests, and a supported ladder. There are twenty side
passages: the first four are 24 long, the remaining sixteen are 25 long.
The central corridor is three wide; passages are one wide and two high. The
five-by-five depot alone has three-high clearance for upper chest lids.

Wall torches are included as a supplied lighting layout along mined passages.
Their backing is normally preserved rock. Runtime code must check the actual
support and hazards before placement. The file is a geometry plan, so do not
paste all passages before workers mine them. The separate landing is a subset
of the full plan; the four-block shaft file is a reusable extension component.
None of these adds or replaces the already-existing mine surface building.

Exact aging budgets for Lumina sources remain gameplay balance values in the
full prompt, not schematic data. The supplied structures implement the agreed
planting space and preserve all tree-related rules.
