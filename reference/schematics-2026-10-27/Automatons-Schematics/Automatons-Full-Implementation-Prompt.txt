Minecraft 1.20.1 / Forge 47.4.10

Implement the complete expansion below in the existing Automatons repository. This is one coordinated update, not a request for a proposal or a partial scaffold. Read AGENTS.md and the relevant source, assets, build scripts, and project documentation first. Preserve working systems unless a requirement below changes them. 2. OWNERSHIP, PROFESSIONS, AND NETWORK RULES

Retain the existing pedestal, ownership, naming, wand, claim protection, network registration, inventory, construction, recall, and persistence systems. Integrate the new work into them rather than creating a second unrelated colony system.

Each production totem supports at most SIX assigned ordinary workers. Guardington protection assignments do not consume these six places. Do not count an Operatorton assigned directly to a Machine as a worker at an unrelated totem.

A totem must have a selected profession before an ordinary worker can be assigned. Enforce this on the server for every entry point, including menus, wand use, packets, and saved assignment restoration. Assigning a compatible ordinary automaton automatically gives them the totem's profession. Remove manual profession selection from the ordinary Automaton GUI.

There are eleven production-totem choices: lumber, mining, farming, transport, building, hunting, crafting, fishing, ranching, quarrying, and Lumina service. Guardingtons use protection assignments instead of production assignments. Operatortons receive their profession through the existing Machine assignment workflow.

An assigned building does NOT have to be complete before workers can be assigned. In particular, preserve the first Buildington's ability to bootstrap a workshop from a configured totem and partial supplies.

Reassignment preserves identity, name, ownership, health, energy, inventory, and valid equipment. Return incompatible equipment to available cargo or linked storage without losing it. Release old task claims and reservations. A profession change must never grant free operating energy or replenish a service reserve.

Guardingtons are a distinct armored body type. An ordinary automaton cannot become a Guardington through assignment, and a Guardington cannot become another profession. Assigning a guard to protect a production totem does not change their profession.

Keep current cargo capacities and equipment architecture. The existing maximum cargo capacity is eighteen, with the existing equipment slots. Do not expand inventories to solve logistics or grave storage. Add job-appropriate validation for bows, swords, rods, shears, and ammunition; keep supplies such as torches, arrows where appropriate, buckets, and leads in suitable existing slots or cargo. Every item must have a real inventory location.

Preserve owner/network isolation and protected-area restrictions. A worker must not harvest from another player's protected worksite or withdraw from unauthorized storage. No chunk forcing, offline simulation, or broad global entity scans.

3.4 Farming crop selectors

Use three large, horizontally aligned ghost/sample slots labeled Left, Middle, and Right. Clicking with a supported crop or seed selects the crop; it does not consume the sample or become the planting supply. Provide an obvious clear action and reject unsupported samples with a short explanation.

Accepted selections: wheat or wheat seeds -> wheat; carrot -> carrots; potato -> potatoes; beetroot or beetroot seeds -> beetroots. Actual planting consumes the correct seeds or vegetables from real stock.

Each selector controls one 3x9 strip of the 9x9 field. Repeated selections are valid. A blank selector disables planting in that strip. On a changed selection, harvest the existing crop when mature, then replant with the new selection. Do not rip up immature crops solely because the selection changed.

4. LUMINA, LUMINALL, AND UPKEEP

4.1 Conversion and collectors

Set the shared conversion to exactly TWO mB of Luminall per ONE point of Lumina:
- 250 mB bottle = 125 Lumina.
- 1,000 mB bucket = 500 Lumina.
- 8,000 mB Tank = 4,000 Lumina.

Apply this consistently across player drinking, automatons, the Machine, portable containers, and Lumina-to-fluid conversion such as the Lumina Drain. Preserve capacities unless explicitly changed here: Tank 8,000 mB, Collector 4,000 mB, and the Machine's existing 10,000-point operating capacity. Do not confuse fluid capacity with Lumina capacity.

Keep exact accounting. Do not round one mB up to one Lumina. Retain unusable fluid remainders where the existing partial-transfer system supports them. If the Lumina Drain spends five points, its new corresponding fluid output is ten mB.

An automaton drinks a WHOLE bottle and returns one empty glass bottle. Clamp the energy gain to the appropriate capacity and discard the excess. This waste is intentional. Do not apply this special full-bottle-waste rule to the Machine's existing partial-container and overflow-retention behavior.

Reduce Collector production to ONE QUARTER of its original rate, including the original capped result. The original maximum was 20 mB per five seconds; the new maximum is 5 mB per five seconds. Apply the quarter-rate scaling once, not repeatedly. Use fractional accumulation so low-yield plants do not become permanently zero-output due to integer truncation. Preserve existing overlap sharing, source recognition, full-tank pausing, and real fluid transfers. Plant/crop sources follow the exhaustion rule in section 4.4. Collection from trees remains non-destructive: COLLECTORS CANNOT KILL TREES.

Collector recipe, using the existing Infused Lumite Shard item and an EMPTY Tank:
Stone | Infused Shard | Stone
Empty | Tank          | Empty
Stone | Infused Shard | Stone

That is four Stone, two Infused Shards, and one empty Tank. Do not use charged cores in this recipe. A filled Tank must not be consumed by crafting. Preserve the Tank recipe of eight Glass around an Empty Core.

4.2 Operating charge

Use a 200-point operating capacity for standard automatons, based on the two 100-point cores in their torso. Guardingtons retain this operating capacity because their armored recipes retain the core requirements. A Luminariton has the same operating capacity plus the separate reserve below.

Every automaton loses 20 operating Lumina per 24,000 ELAPSED SIMULATION TICKS: deduct one point per 1,200 active server ticks. Preserve the partial interval across saves. Sleeping, commands that change world time, and day boundaries must not cause additional deductions. Idle workers still consume energy; powered-down workers do not go negative. Do not charge unloaded entities or perform offline catch-up.

At or below 1% operating charge, suspend work safely and return to the assigned shed/worksite recharge point. Guardingtons use their assigned protected site, or an accessible owned network site when on circuit patrol. Operatortons stop/park the Machine safely and use the existing access route before returning. Unassigned workers use an accessible owned pedestal/network supply point.

Finish the recharge behavior as follows: consume one available full bottle, then resume once above the 1% threshold. There is no requirement to fill to 100% before working. Accept manual feeding at any non-full charge, always consuming the full bottle and wasting overflow. If no bottle is available, request supplies and wait rather than repeatedly wandering between empty stores.

At zero charge, stop all work and movement. Eyes go black, head slumps forward, and arms hang down. Persist this state. Adding a bottle restores energy and allows normal resumption. Drinking Luminall never directly heals hearts.

Do not silently refill an automaton's operating charge from mining, tree cutting, or flower breaking. Preserve any existing player-facing Lumina resource behavior without turning ordinary work into an unrequested substitute for bottled upkeep.

4.3 Luminariton reserve and healing: final implementation defaults

Give each Luminariton a separate 400-point SERVICE RESERVE, in addition to their 200-point operating charge. The reserve starts empty when newly entering the profession and must be funded from real Luminall. Preserve it without duplication if changing jobs; never refill it for free by reassignment. Keep any stored reserve dormant while not a Luminariton rather than copying it into operating charge.

The service reserve pays for healing and shard infusion. This resolves the remaining energy-source detail without adding another resource system. Existing shard infusion still costs 25 Lumina per shard.

Healing defaults:
- Range: four blocks from the Luminariton, using actual distance rather than the totem's range.
- Rate: one health point, or half a heart, per second for each eligible nearby damaged automaton.
- Cost: two service-reserve Lumina per health point restored.
- Heal living automatons in the same ownership/network context, including the Luminariton themselves when damaged.
- Spend energy only for actual health restored, including proportional handling of a fractional final health point. Use exact fixed-point accounting if needed.
- If reserve is scarce, prioritize the lowest health percentage and use stable fair ordering for ties. Never overspend or duplicate reserve through simultaneous recipients.
- Reuse the Infusing Pedestal's Lumina-transfer visual language, with energy visibly drawn from the mage to the recipient.

A worker at or below half health seeks an available owned Luminariton when safe. Nearby damaged workers can receive healing without explicitly starting a visit. Retreat from active danger takes precedence. Visitors use the ground-level clearing, including Guardingtons; they do not climb into the workshop. Healing does not refill operating charge. A dead or powered-down Luminariton emits no healing.

Prioritize the Luminariton's own operating energy, then healing, then other service tasks. When the service reserve falls to 125 points or lower, refill toward 400 from actual full bottles in workshop stock as available, using normal bottle-use timing and the full-bottle overflow rule. Do not consume one bottle twice or apply one bottle to both meters. Filling bottles, maintaining collectors, and fetching supplies must remain possible so a low reserve cannot deadlock the service.

4.4 Fully drained plants and crops

Use the mod's EXISTING Lumina-source eligibility rules as the authority. Inspect the collection/harvest code and its tags/configuration, and reuse those rules rather than introducing a broader list of plants merely because they can be rendered or tinted. Being a plant, flower, or crop by itself does not make a block a Lumina source. Preserve existing maturity and other source-validity requirements.

For an eligible non-tree plant or crop, track a finite source reserve against actual Lumina extracted. Reuse existing depletion accounting if present; otherwise add persistent accounting and centralized, configurable starting reserves, documenting the chosen balance. Do not confuse a harvest yield or per-cycle production rate with an already agreed lifetime reserve. Shared Collectors debit one source reserve only for the output actually produced, using the settled 1 Lumina to 2 mB conversion. A full or paused Collector causes no drain, and collection cannot extract beyond the remaining reserve. Preserve depletion through save/unload/reload and ordinary crop growth; treat both halves of a tall plant as one source.

Once that reserve reaches zero, replace the source in place with one of TWO new plantable blocks: Dead Plant for a non-crop plant, or Dead Crop for a crop. Give them simple static dead-vegetation artwork and corresponding placeable block items. Healthy sources keep their ordinary appearance until the replacement occurs. Do not implement progressive color fading, tint stages, wilting, or drooping in this update.

The new dead blocks produce ZERO Lumina, do not grow, and cannot be revived by bonemeal. Replacement must not award the original healthy plant's harvest drops. Clearing them yields only their own dead-plant/dead-crop item, never a healthy flower, viable seed, or normal crop yield. Respect suitable support blocks, including farmland for Dead Crop. When replacing a tall source, clear its other half safely without duplicate remains or drops. Do not classify these new dead blocks as harvestable living flowers or active Lumina sources.

TREES ARE EXEMPT. Preserve their existing eligibility and collection behavior, including rooted-tree recognition and structural-log rejection. Collector extraction must never kill a tree, remove or replace its logs, leaves, or roots, or apply the Dead Plant/Dead Crop conversion to any part of it. This exemption does not add new tree sources or change existing yields.

Protected Collector beds remain susceptible to plant/crop exhaustion. Their protection blocks opportunistic picking; it does not prevent Collector drain or their assigned Luminariton's deliberate clearing of dead vegetation and replanting from real supplies.

5. SHARED FLOWER COLLECTION AND SUPPLY LOGISTICS

Every automaton profession opportunistically harvests flowers it passes within TWO BLOCKS of while following its current route. Do not create a flower-search job, change their destination, or make them detour. The action should not interrupt fighting, escaping immediate danger, ladder safety, or powered-down behavior.

Use a bounded, throttled nearby check, with a default of at most one flower harvest per ten ticks. Only break a flower when its normal drops can fit in inventory. Resolve the actual drops once and preserve normal tall-flower behavior without duplicating both halves. Use appropriate flower tags and compatible modded flowers. Do not classify crops, grass, saplings, or every decorative plant as flowers.

Unload flowers into normal network storage. Runnertons supply eligible flowers and crop planting materials to Luminaritons, who maintain their registered Collector plots under sections 4.4 and 6.6. Replanting always consumes actual supplies; dead vegetation cannot be used as a living source or planting substitute.

Managed Collector beds are PROTECTED from this opportunistic harvesting, including harvesting by their own Luminariton. Persist the protected plot geometry independently of the flower blocks so a temporary empty cell does not lose protection. Respect the existing protected-area system as well. Do not quietly exempt every other player-planted flower; the agreed exception is the protected Collector beds.

Expand Runnerton request/delivery support to include the supplies these jobs need: tools, rods, bows/swords where supported, arrows, torches, safe fill blocks, breeding food, shears, buckets, leads, flowers, eligible plant/crop planting supplies, bottles, Luminall containers, fuel, raw shards, infused shards, and construction materials. Remove any old intentional exclusion that would prevent these deliveries. Preserve filters and owner restrictions, reserve items atomically, and avoid hauling cycles or stealing a worker's in-use equipment.

Do not add special mining-only logistics rules or automatic capacity scaling. Storage pressure is intentional. Players balance the network by providing storage and enough Runnertons. Full storage pauses production cleanly until capacity is available.

6. PROFESSION IMPLEMENTATION

6.1 Hunterton

Use a fixed 96-block horizontal radius centered on the assigned HUNTING TOTEM. Do not center the hunt on the pedestal or on the moving worker. Use normal loaded-world pathfinding, without loading distant chunks just to find prey.

Eligible prey: adult passive animal entities, including appropriately classified/tagged modded animals. Always exclude babies, horses and the equine family, cats, dogs/wolves, and livestock with the persistent managed-livestock protection flag. Villagers, other non-animal NPCs, players, and automatons are not prey. A friendly or owned pet must never become a hunting target merely because it is a passive Animal subclass.

Define the shared managed-livestock flag now and persist it with the entity. Ranchertons apply it to their livestock. Protection remains if an animal escapes the ranch. Recheck eligibility during pursuit and immediately before attacking. Drop a target that becomes protected or leaves the hunting area. The flag prevents hunting; it does not grant general invulnerability or prevent the managing Rancherton's authorized adult-culling operation. No fence-detection workaround is required.

Support bow, real arrows, and sword; use normal item damage, durability, and applicable enchantments. Visibly switch held weapons. Use a bow with line of sight at range, and a sword when close or out of ammunition. They can continue with either usable weapon. If neither is usable, return for equipment and wait if stock is missing.

Reserve targets across hunters. Avoid shots through players, friendly automatons, and excluded/protected animals. Collect normal death drops without duplicating them. Return to the hunting shed to unload when needed and restock arrows from its chests. Hunters are roamers and can retreat into any suitable owned automaton shed when threatened.

6.2 Crafterton

Craftertons automatically know supported recipes. Do not add recipe teaching or learning. Support ordinary crafting-table recipes and actual furnace and blast-furnace processing, including compatible modded recipes using those recipe types. Consume real ingredients and fuel, honor processing time and remainders, and use real station input/output inventories.

Resolve supported ingredient dependencies with bounded cycle detection and inventory reservations. Choose workable recipes without consuming equipped tools or valuable custom items as generic ingredients when an ordinary alternative is available. Unsupported machine-specific recipes should produce a concise Unsupported recipe status and leave the request intact.

Order priority is strictly:
1. Tools and required equipment.
2. Torches.
3. Materials requested by Buildington construction.
4. Other supported network work orders.
5. Restocking the display armor stand, only after pending work orders.

If a Runnerton exists in the same network, the Crafterton stays in the workshop. Couriers fetch ingredients and deliver outputs. Delayed deliveries mean waiting or doing another eligible order, not abandoning the workshop to collect materials. Without a Runnerton, the Crafterton can collect and distribute materials themselves.

At the approved B6 armor stand, maintain exactly one full set of ordinary iron armor: helmet, chestplate, leggings, and boots. Fill the initial set, then replace only an empty piece slot after the player removes that piece. Do not replace occupied slots, produce duplicate full sets, or allow Runnertons to loot the display. This remains the lowest-priority work.

Luminall bottling and shard infusion belong to Luminariton service in this update. Do not create a competing free-energy shortcut in the crafting job.

6.3 Fisherton

Reserve one of six fixed fishing positions per worker on the assigned dock. Fill positions from the far end inward, with one worker on either side in each of three pairs. The center walkway remains unobstructed. Each pair has two blocks of dock length.

Some Fishertons stand and some sit. Choose a stable visual variant until the worker leaves the occupied dock slot, rather than changing it every tick or cast. Chairs simply appear as part of the fishing presentation; they are not inventory items, real placed blocks, construction ingredients, or extra loot. Remove the chair visual when the worker leaves, dies, unloads, or changes state.

Use real fishing rods, appropriate timing, durability, and enchantments. Generate catches from Minecraft's fishing loot tables with a valid fishing context, including fish, junk, and treasure when their normal conditions permit. Respect compatible loot-table changes. Do not hardcode a list of fish or force treasure eligibility. Preserve actual biome/weather/open-water behavior through the supported vanilla/Forge mechanisms. Do not abuse the owner's player inventory or statistics to simulate the worker.

Require a contiguous 10-block-long by 7-block-wide area of SOURCE WATER beneath and around the 3x8 dock. The three-wide dock is centered across the seven-wide water area, leaving two water columns on each side and two rows beyond the end. Flowing water does not satisfy the required cells. A 3x3 pool is invalid. Each assigned position also needs a usable casting location. This dimension check does not override vanilla loot eligibility.

Collect catches, unload into the shore-hut storage, request replacement rods, and cooperate with Runnertons. Fishertons do not sleep at night; they retreat when threatened like other ordinary workers.

6.4 Rancherton

The PLAYER brings initial animals into the ranch. Ranchertons do not roam to round up wild animals. Their managed area is the actual fenced pen connected to the shed, not a circular world radius.

Manage sheep, cows, chickens, and pigs. Register eligible animals inside the pen, persist the managed-livestock protection flag, and use shared animal/task claims so multiple ranch workers do not perform the same action twice. Escaped animals keep hunting protection, but the player is responsible for returning them. Count animals currently in the pen or in an active shed-handling task for population management.

Jobs:
- Shear adult sheep with real shears and normal durability/drop behavior. Respect wool regrowth and keep suitable grass in the pen.
- Milk adult cows using actual buckets and deposit registered milk fluid into a compatible tank. Return/reuse the empty bucket. Default cooldown: one bucket per cow per 24,000 elapsed ticks, persisted independently of skipped nights.
- Collect dropped eggs.
- Breed sheep and cows with wheat, chickens with wheat seeds, and pigs with carrots. Request these through the network. Use normal breeding cooldowns, consume actual food, and reserve parents and the expected offspring count.
- Cull surplus ADULTS by leading one into the shed, closing the gate appropriately, performing one instant humane dispatch action, and collecting normal drops.

Culling needs no weapon or additional tool item. Never perform repeated visible attacks. Use the normal death/drop pipeline once, with an appropriate ranch action, and never kill babies. Preserve at least two adults of a managed species as breeding stock. Use a real reusable lead without duplication or loss through the handling/death sequence.

Population defaults: target EIGHT animals per species, counting adults and babies. Allow targets from 2 through 64 and a separate management enable/disable toggle for each species. Disabled population management stops breeding and culling for that species without removing its protection or preventing appropriate collection tasks. Do not breed when the existing count plus reserved offspring would exceed the target. Cull surplus adults only while respecting the breeding pair and baby protections. Imported excess babies remain until adulthood; do not force the target by killing them.

Skip production actions if products or container remainders cannot be stored. Show a concrete shortage/full-storage reason. Preserve gates and contain animals while moving between pen and shed.

Generalize the Tank to accept any properly registered fluid, one fluid type at a time, with normal bucket/automation capabilities. Use an existing compatible milk fluid where available or register one correctly for this mod's use. Do not mix fluids or silently replace existing contents. Preserve normal Tank break/spill behavior and wand-packed fluid retention.

6.5 Quarryton

Quarrying is a separate profession with a fixed 15x15 horizontal excavation footprint. Remove quarry operation from Miningtons. Never expand the quarry beyond the registered footprint to follow ores or caves.

Work downward in complete horizontal layers, using persistent row/cell claims so multiple Quarrytons cooperate. Skip unbreakable blocks without destroying them. Keep the ladder shaft and its backing wall outside the excavation, centered just beyond the edge as specified in the building plan. Extend access downward as work progresses.

Default depth is bedrock/the lowest safely reachable excavation level in that dimension. Provide an optional higher bottom-Y setting. Do not hardcode the Overworld's minimum Y, break bedrock, or tunnel around a bedrock-blocked shaft. Never mark a layer complete just because supplies, paths, chunks, or storage are unavailable.

Plug discovered water and lava with real ordinary safe full blocks from inventory: cobblestone, dirt, cobbled deepslate/deepslate, tuff, andesite, and comparable explicitly allowed materials. Do not spend ores, precious blocks, or unsupported falling blocks as filler. One placed block consumes one item. If filler is missing, request it and pause safely.

Quarrytons must carry torches and progressively light access and work surfaces. Use a conservative default target of at least eight block light on working floor/access locations where placement permits, with obstruction-aware placement. If lighting supplies run out, stop extending into an unlit work area, request torches, and return safely. Existing adequate lighting should not cause pointless duplicate torch placement.

A newly discovered cave pauses excavation, raises a visible worksite help alert, and sends workers back to the surface. The player can make the opening safe and acknowledge Resume. Persist acknowledged cave discoveries so the identical opening does not immediately retrigger the alert, while newly encountered openings still do. This does not authorize mining outside the quarry boundary.

Guardingtons DO NOT descend into quarries. If attacked, Quarrytons retreat up the ladder. Lighting and a usable escape route are required parts of their work. Use the eight-double-chest surface store and normal Runnerton logistics.

6.6 Luminariton

Implement the service job with these responsibilities: tend registered Collectors, fill bottles and tanks from actual fluid, maintain operating/service charge, infuse supplied raw Lumite shards, plant and maintain eligible non-tree plants/crops around Collectors, replace fully exhausted dead vegetation, and heal network automatons using the reserve rules above. The approved default courtyard remains a flower garden.

Use the raised workshop, ground-level totem, open lower tower, rear ladder/open hatch, and large courtyard specified in the structures section. The mage can climb to workshop equipment and come down to the collectors and healing clearing. Ordinary workers and couriers can use the ladder; Guardingtons remain at ground level.

Bottling consumes actual empty glass bottles and 250 mB per full bottle. Infusing consumes actual raw shards and 25 service-reserve Lumina per shard. Filling tanks transfers fluid with exact accounting. Plants and crop planting items are taken from real storage and used only in suitable protected plot cells; do not plant onto paths, in the healing clearing, on a Collector, or outside the registered plots. Use only the mod's existing eligible Lumina-source plants/crops, with valid soil, farmland, irrigation, and clearance as applicable. Do not plant a crop into an unsuitable flower-bed cell or silently redesign the approved courtyard. Handle tall flower placement and clearance correctly if those are used.

Luminaritons NEVER plant trees, saplings, mangrove propagules, or other tree-producing planting stock, and do not grow or bonemeal such stock into trees. Existing tree planting by other appropriate professions remains unchanged. Collectors may continue drawing from eligible trees already present without harming them.

Clear Dead Plant/Dead Crop blocks in managed plots and replant from actual supplies. If replacement stock is missing, request it and leave the cell awaiting supplies; never turn a dead item into a free living plant. Keep bed protection while a cell is empty. Do not remove healthy plants to continually rearrange the garden. A partially supplied garden should begin operating with the plants and collectors already available, then fill missing positions as supplies arrive.

6.7 Guardington

Create a separate Guardington body with an actual collision size of 1.4 blocks wide and 2.5 blocks tall. Match the model's stance and proportions. Do not squeeze it through standard doors. 8. HOSTILE MOBS, RETREAT, AND DEATH

8.1 Threat behavior

Automatons do not sleep or stop work merely because it is night. They retreat when danger appears. Add automaton targets to these exact hostile entity types: zombies, skeletons, spiders, and cave spiders, under their appropriate normal aggression conditions. Do not broadly target all Monster subclasses or silently include every zombie/skeleton variant.

Other mobs ignore automatons for automatic targeting, including creepers. Do not make creepers acquire automatons as living targets or make guards routinely provoke creepers. Environmental damage, crossfire, fire, lava, explosions, and other ordinary hazards can still hurt automatons.

Guards defend against the specified threats. Ordinary workers prioritize escape to an accessible safe owned shed. Hunters may use any suitable owned shed instead of insisting on their own distant hut. Preserve paths home, close appropriate doors/gates after entry, and do not grant magical invulnerability inside a structure. Workers resume their saved work once the danger clears and conditions permit.

Powered-down automatons remain physical, damageable entities. Zero energy does not grant immunity and does not itself create a grave.

8.2 Death recovery

On actual death, create a gravestone with one ordinary 27-slot chest immediately beneath it. Store all carried inventory, tools, equipment, and the recovered body parts. Use the user's established inventory limits so this fits one small chest. Do not add a larger custom grave inventory or silently discard overflow if an invariant is violated.

Recover three, four, or five physical body parts, with a small chance of all six. Final distribution: 30% three, 35% four, 30% five, and 5% six. The TORSO ALWAYS survives. Select the remainder without exceeding the actual body: one head, one torso, two arms, and two legs. Guards drop the corresponding armored versions. Two recovered arms are two parts even if they stack into one inventory slot.

Recovered parts do not preserve the old automaton's identity, name, ownership binding, job state, or experience. Rebuilding creates a new individual. A fully recovered body is still a rebuild, not resurrection.

The grave chest may occupy a death-location block even in lava. Do not automatically relocate a lava death to a safe shore. NEVER replace bedrock. Prefer the death position and immediately beneath it, searching only as needed for a valid nearby non-bedrock position. Also avoid destroying an existing valuable block entity or container and losing its contents; preserve or safely defer recovery if placement cannot be committed atomically. Record a pending grave rather than voiding the inventory. The gravestone sits above the resulting chest.

Handle all death causes and double-callback cases without duplicate graves or duplicate drops. Remove the dead worker from assignments, claims, dock slots, patrol spacing, and pending deliveries. Preserve recoverable item NBT and container contents. The death handler must not also scatter the same items as ordinary entity drops.
