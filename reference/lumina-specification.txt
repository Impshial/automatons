The progression is: mine Lumite for shards; use shards in the Empty Core recipe; infuse individual shards at the Infusing Pedestal; craft one Empty Core plus four Infused Lumite Shards into one Lumina Core; use cores and infused shards in the appropriate existing recipes.

Add appropriate mineable/pickaxe, tool-tier, and Forge ore tags. Make Miningtons discover, mine, and collect Lumite through their existing terrain/ore logic, with normal protection, reach, mining time, Fortune/Silk Touch, inventory and drop handling. Do not introduce a special remote-mining or item-spawning shortcut.

5. REDESIGN THE INFUSING PEDESTAL

Make the pedestal larger than its current half-height design: a full-size single-block pedestal with a black-and-white body and four clearly visible raised corner prongs. Keep the silhouette, collision shape, item model, and interaction geometry coherent. Retain Minecraft pixel density and do not create an elaborate multiblock structure.

It has no GUI. It contains exactly four physical infusion positions, one per corner, and each position accepts ONE Lumite Shard, not a whole stack.

Interactions:
- Right-click a corner with raw shards to insert one into that empty position, decrementing the held stack correctly.
- Make corner selection predictable from the block hit position and viewing angle. The four prongs should make the positions obvious.
- Right-click an occupied corner with an empty hand to retrieve its shard. Partial progress must survive retrieval and reinsertion; a completed shard is retrieved as an Infused Lumite Shard.
- An occupied corner cannot be overwritten. A full pedestal gives brief feedback without consuming anything.
- Server authority determines insertion, withdrawal, progress, payment, and completion. Client animation cannot create or charge an item.
- Preserve existing interaction permission rules. A player's shard must not draw energy from unrelated nearby players.

Visuals:
- Each inserted shard immediately appears floating above its own corner and continuously rotates. Keep it anchored above that corner rather than orbiting the whole pedestal.
- Occupied corners can infuse simultaneously.
- Show white-purple Lumina flowing from the contributing player toward each actively charging shard.
- Only show active transfer while real energy is being paid. No transfer effect when the player is absent, out of range, empty of Lumina, or the shard is complete.
- A completed shard emits green completion particles from its corner and remains floating/rotating until retrieved.
- Keep particles and synchronization bounded, including with several pedestals nearby. Use the project's current infusion sound conventions where appropriate.

6. INFUSION COST AND OWNERSHIP

Infusing ONE shard costs exactly 25 Lumina. Four shards cost exactly 100 Lumina. Neither parallel charging nor partial insertion changes that cost.

Each corner stores the inserted item, exact paid progress, and contributing player's stable identity. Draw only from the player who inserted that shard while they are nearby and in the same dimension. Reuse the current infusion proximity distance unless it is defective; document the actual value. With shards inserted by different players, each contributes only to their own shards.

Use the existing exact fractional-energy accounting. Never round the charge independently each tick in a way that creates a discount or overcharge. Pay only the remaining cost, stop at exactly 25, and perform one item conversion on completion.

Retain the current pedestal's total transfer-rate budget as an initial default and distribute it fairly among eligible active corners. Four occupied positions must not accidentally multiply the configured total drain rate by four. Document the effective rate and resulting single-shard/full-batch times. Simultaneous infusion means each eligible shard advances, not that one corner monopolizes all available energy.

If the player runs out of Lumina, leaves range, disconnects, or the chunk unloads, pause and preserve progress. Resume when conditions allow. Completed corners consume no energy. Do not drain the player below zero.

Partial progress belongs to the item and must survive inventory movement, pedestal removal, and reinsertion without duplication. Represent partially infused items safely so their progress cannot spread to an entire stack of untouched shards. Only the completed Infused Lumite Shard item satisfies infused-ingredient recipes.

Breaking or packing the pedestal drops/returns its real contents once through the established block lifecycle. Save and reload must preserve all four positions. Handle player inventories with no room using the existing safe transfer/drop behavior. Include multiplayer attempts to retrieve or insert into the same corner in validation.
