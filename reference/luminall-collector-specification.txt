Implement a **Lumina Collector** and **Luminall fluid** in the existing Automatons mod.

First inspect the project’s current Minecraft/Forge version, Lumina system, Infused Core items, producer detection, block registration, rendering, and fluid infrastructure. Reuse existing systems and conventions. Complete the implementation, including assets, recipes, persistence, and validation.

**Purpose**

The Lumina Collector is a passive, single-block generator placed amongst flowers, pumpkins, melons, crops, trees, and anything else the mod recognizes as a Lumina producer.

It slowly draws Lumina from nearby producers and condenses it into **Luminall**, the liquid form of Lumina. Collection leaves the surrounding plants intact.

The collector requires no fuel or external power. Its expensive crafting recipe is the investment required for ongoing production. Players can install multiple collectors to increase production across a larger area.

**Crafting**

The recipe must include **four Infused Cores**.

Use the existing infused/charged core item in the project. Do not substitute Infused Shards or empty cores, and do not create a duplicate item if the correct core already exists under a different registry name.

Choose appropriate existing materials for the remaining recipe slots and report the completed recipe.

**Collection range**

Use a **4×4-block horizontal collection footprint**. This means four blocks wide by four blocks long.

Because the footprint has even dimensions, anchor it consistently relative to the collector and document the exact offsets. The displayed boundary must match the positions actually scanned.

Vertical reach has not been finalized. Use a modest, configurable default that allows nearby trees and plants at different elevations to contribute, and report the chosen value.

Looking at a collector while holding the Automaton Wand should display its collection boundary. Integrate this with any existing range visualization system.

**Lumina production**

Use the mod’s existing definition of Lumina-producing blocks wherever possible. Keep producer eligibility and output extensible through the project’s existing configuration or data-driven conventions.

Flowers, pumpkins, melons, crops, and living trees must be supported. Recognize planted vegetation as well as naturally generated vegetation. Reuse existing tree recognition where available so wooden buildings do not function as living trees.

More eligible producers should increase output, up to a configurable maximum collection rate. Crop maturity can affect contribution where the existing producer system supports it.

For overlapping collectors, share each producer’s available output between collectors. Placing additional collectors around the same plant must not multiply that plant’s production. A collector that cannot accept more fluid should not prevent another collector from using the available production.

Run collection on the server. Cache or periodically refresh producer information instead of scanning the entire collection volume every tick. Do not force-load surrounding chunks.

**Internal reservoir**

Give the collector an internal Luminall tank.

It continues collecting without a bottle until the tank is full. It resumes when space becomes available through bottle filling, bucket collection, or pipe extraction.

Persist the tank contents and bottle state across saves, chunk unloading, and server restarts. Synchronize the information needed for client rendering.

Choose a useful, configurable tank capacity.

**Bottle placement and drinking**

Right-clicking the collector with a glass bottle places one bottle physically on top of the block.

The bottle must be visible in the world and visibly fill with Luminall. Use actual stored fluid for filling and deduct the corresponding amount from the collector.

Right-clicking with an empty hand retrieves the bottle. Handle partial filling consistently, preserving its fluid value when the bottle is removed or the collector is broken.

A full **Luminall Bottle** is drinkable. Drinking it adds a fixed, configurable amount of Lumina to the player’s existing Lumina bank and returns an empty glass bottle.

Use the existing Lumina storage, synchronization, and capacity rules. Drinking must work regardless of hunger.

Choose and document a fixed fluid volume per bottle. Keep that volume consistent across all filling and consumption paths. Partially filled bottles must not grant the benefit of a full bottle.

**Fully registered Luminall fluid**

Luminall must be a complete registered fluid with:

- A source form and a flowing form.
- A placeable fluid block.
- A Luminall Bucket holding the standard 1,000 mB.
- Working bucket placement and source pickup.
- Still and flowing textures, appropriate tinting, and localization.
- Compatibility with standard Forge fluid containers, tanks, and transfer systems.

Disable infinite-source regeneration. Moving, placing, collecting, or bottling Luminall must conserve fluid quantity.

Use the project’s established registration and client rendering patterns.

**Fluid extraction and automation**

Expose the collector’s reservoir through the standard Forge fluid capability so compatible pipes can pump Luminall into external tanks or machines.

Allow extraction from the sides and bottom, keeping the top available for the visible bottle.

Support manual bucket collection when at least 1,000 mB is available.

Pipe extraction, bucket collection, and bottle filling must use consistent server-side accounting. Simultaneous interactions must not duplicate fluid, produce negative amounts, or consume more than is available.

**Appearance and interaction**

Use the mod’s established Lumina colors for the fluid and effects.

Tooltips should describe function concisely. Crafting instructions belong in recipe displays.
