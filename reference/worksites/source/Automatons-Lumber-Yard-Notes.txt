LUMBER YARD: OPEN TIMBER SHELTER

Compact worksite with an open front and sides, enclosed rear wall, single-slope
spruce slab roof, two double chests, a small decorative log rack and chopping stump.
An item frame holding a stone axe identifies the front entrance.
- Main foundation: 7 x 7. Roof/eave reservation: 9 x 9.
- Schematic dimensions: 9 X x 6 Y x 9 Z. Front is negative Z.
- Lean-to roof slopes from the high front toward the low back. It has no ridge.
  Top surface: Y=5.5 at Z=0..2, Y=5 at Z=3..5, Y=4.5 at Z=6..8.
  Perimeter infill closes support junctions. Maximum height is 5.5 blocks
  including the foundation. Its two roof steps are each half a block.
- Totem and paste/construction anchor: (4,1,7), exposed at rear-wall bottom center.
- WEOffset=(-4,-1,-7); bury foundation at Y=0 so walking level Y=1 is flush.
- Frame tile: (4,3,0), north-facing, attached to front beam at (4,3,1).
- Two double chests: X=2 and X=6, Y=1, paired Z=5,6, facing toward the center.
  Each has clear lid space and a neighboring access lane.
- Main aisle: X=4, Z=1..6, with two full blocks clear under the front beam.
- Log rack: (2,1,2), (2,1,3), (2,2,3); chopping stump: (6,1,3).
  These are four placed oak logs, not four inventories or a new processing feature.
- Two entrance lanterns plus two interior wall torches light the shelter.
- No door is needed for the open front; the center aisle leads straight to the totem.
- Additional chests may be placed within the current storage range, with access
  kept clear. This starting layout supplies four chest items forming two doubles.
- Requires Automatons for automatons:totem and block entity automatons:station.

HARVESTING EXCLUSION
The Network must register every building's actual structural block positions,
including log frames, racks and stumps, and exclude them from Lumberton targets.
Include reserved construction positions before the first block is placed, so
workers cannot harvest a half-built structure. Keep this exclusion local to
building contents/blueprints; do not protect the entire surrounding chunk or
disable normal tree harvesting throughout the work radius. Other registered
worksites need the same structural-log protection. The schematic itself cannot
enforce this rule; it must be implemented in the mod's worksite/target validation.

FORMAT AND IMPORT
Minecraft Java 1.20.1, DataVersion 3465, Sponge Schematic v2.
All coordinates below refer to the exported schematic, unless labeled body coordinates.
Every building is anchored to the player-placed totem, which remains exposed
and usable in the completed structure. All future buildings must follow this rule.
The builder derives the footprint, entrance, floor and placements from that
totem's position and the worksite's chosen orientation. Do not choose another
center and then relocate the totem. Save orientation and reserved bounds before
construction so retries cannot rotate or shift an existing project.
WorldEdit origin is stored in WEOffset and Offset=(0,0,0). Foundation blocks go
one block below normal walking level, so the entrance is flush with the ground.
Load the file by its basename with //schem load, stand at the specified anchor,
and use //paste on a disposable test site. Entity pasting must be enabled.
Do not paste a template over an operational worksite or an assigned totem.

ITEM FRAME
The identifier is a real minecraft:item_frame entity in the schematic's Entities
list, not a block or a block entity. It is north-facing, visible, removable,
not invulnerable and contains one real item. It carries no fixed UUID.
When importing this template into the mod's construction system, process its
Entities list as well as blocks. Place the support block first; then create the
frame and insert the displayed item, consuming one frame and one display item.
Transform position, hanging tile, support and facing together when rotating.
Do not double-charge or duplicate the frame or display item after retries or
reload. Identification is cosmetic: changing the display must not change the
totem's purpose. Do not take a worker's active tool to fill a display frame.
Resource totals include the frame and its display item. Door halves consume
one door item together. Each chest block consumes one chest item. Double slabs
consume two slab items, and wall torches consume ordinary torch items.

CONSTRUCTION
Reserve the occupied site and approach, respect protection and existing work,
and validate support, access and supplies before placing. Build from real
inventory with persistent progress. Schematic air is not blanket permission to
erase terrain, player buildings or containers. Preserve the current totem's
identity, links, owner, purpose, upgrades, inventory and other saved data by
skipping both block placement AND block-entity loading at the existing totem.
Registered chest inventories are empty and contain no loot tables. Install
supports before attached lights and frames. Supply the exact rotated chest
facings and pairs. These files do not implement worker behavior or new items.

VALIDATION
Decoded the written NBT and compared every block, block entity, entity and
anchor. Checked frame support and orientation data, chest pairs and lid air,
roof coverage and central access where applicable. Source comparisons prove
the warehouse has no structural changes and the mine adds only eight rear wall
planks and one totem, preserving every original block.
No Minecraft client paste, mob navigation or build-animation test was run here.
Rendered previews use block data; chests, totem and framed item visuals are
illustrative. The actual files contain their named registry IDs.
