FARMER'S SHED

Compact oak farm building with a shallow spruce HIP ROOF sloping on all four
sides, broad windows, a small entrance porch, a composter and a wooden seat.
The roof has no gable and is distinct from the Builder's Shed and Lumber Yard.
A real item frame above the door holds a stone hoe.

DIMENSIONS AND LAYOUT
- Body: 7 x 7 blocks. Interior: 5 x 5, with a clear center aisle.
- Full roof/eave reservation and schematic: 9 X x 7 Y x 9 Z.
- Foundation: local Y=0. Walking level: local Y=1. Maximum roof top: Y=6.5.
- Front faces north (negative Z).
- Existing totem / construction anchor: (4,1,7), exposed in rear wall center.
- WEOffset=(-4,-1,-7). Front door lower half: (4,1,1).
- Frame tile: (4,3,0); supporting front beam: (4,3,1).
- Two double chests: X=2 and X=6, Y=1, paired Z=5,6, facing inward.
- Four wall torches inside; two lanterns under the front eave.
- Five-block porch: X=2..6, Y=0, Z=0. Keep an outside approach to X=4 clear.
- Composter: (2,1,0), empty. Seat: (6,1,0), one ordinary oak stair.
- Register all structure logs as worksite blocks, excluded from lumber targets.

automatic composting behavior or promise a bone-meal supply for Farmingtons.

PLACEMENT AND CONSTRUCTION
Place the totem at normal ground walking level. Build outward from that fixed
anchor, burying the foundation one block so the entrance is flush with terrain.
Reserve the entire 9 x 9 area plus a clear entrance approach before starting.
The already-placed totem is not another material to consume. Preserve its block
entity, identity, owner, links, upgrades, contents, settings and requests.
Skip template placement and block-entity loading at this live anchor.
All chest inventories are empty. Do not overwrite existing occupied containers.
Place supports before attached lights and the frame; handle the door as one
item creating two halves. Double slabs require two slab items each.
The front lanterns hang from the underside of the slab eave.

The hoe sign is a minecraft:item_frame ENTITY in the schematic Entities list.
Import entities as well as blocks, consume a frame plus one stone hoe, rotate
its position/support/facing with the building, and generate a fresh entity UUID.
The display is cosmetic and does not set the totem purpose or worker profession.

FARM RELATIONSHIP
This schematic contains the SHED ONLY. It does not include a crop field, water,
seeds, fencing, paths, or the supplies a Farmington needs for field preparation.
Buildington constructs the shed. Farmingtons tend existing eligible farmland,
or establish one shared 9 x 9 field outside the totem's storage region and
inside its work region. Additional Farmingtons share that field.
The field is reserved separately and must not overlap the shed or its access.
Use the mod's actual radius metric and storage eligibility to register chests.

FORMAT AND INTEGRATION
Minecraft Java 1.20.1, DataVersion 3465, Sponge Schematic v2.

Rotate all block states, entities and relative positions around the totem.
