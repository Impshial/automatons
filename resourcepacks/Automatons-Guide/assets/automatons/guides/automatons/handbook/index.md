# Automaton Handbook

Build a home, give each worksite a purpose, and put your Automatons to work.
Start with [Your first Network](getting-started.md), or use the chapters below.

<SubPages icons={true} />

Hover an associated item in your inventory and hold <KeyBind id="key.guideme.guide" /> to open its page.
The control is **Open Guide for Items**, under **GuideME** in Controls; its default is **G** and it can be rebound.
Use the book's search to find a job, item, or status. All chapters are available immediately and work offline.
Use the toolbar to search, go back, or expand the reading area.
