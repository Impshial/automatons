# Gathering Lumina

Harvest eligible natural logs, crops, fruit, berries, flowers, stems, leaves and grass to release Lumina orbs.
Collect the orbs to increase your separate Lumina balance. Your ordinary experience levels and Mending continue to work normally.

Natural provenance matters: repeatedly placing and breaking resources is not a source of free energy. Immature crops and already-paid resources do not create repeated rewards.
Worker harvesting does not give the worker a personal energy balance. Mining stone or Lumite gives no Lumina.

The Lumina bar displays your balance. Infusion spends only the donor's energy, including exact thirds, without draining nearby bystanders.
If infusion stops, stand within four blocks of your shard and check that your balance is positive.
World settings can change harvest yields and whether Lumina is retained on death.

For a passive garden supply, use a [Lumina Collector](luminall.md). It leaves plants intact and produces Luminall, which can be bottled and drunk to restore Lumina.
