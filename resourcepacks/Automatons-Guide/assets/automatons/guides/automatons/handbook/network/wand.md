# Automaton Wand

Hold the Wand and **sneak-scroll** to switch between **Assign**, **Protect**, **Revoke** and **Wrench**.

<Recipe id="automatons:wand" />

In **Assign**, right-click your worker, then right-click its Totem. Sneak-click cancels selection. A selection expires after a minute; select the worker again if needed.
Use a Totem in the same dimension and home Network.

**Protect** and **Revoke** open a confirmation for the targeted chunk, covering all heights. Protected cells stop worker changes and unsafe felling.
These local protections do not grant permission over another player's claims.

In **Wrench**, sneak-right-click an owned worker to pack it. Its cargo and tools drop separately, once. Normal right-click reminds you of the packing gesture.
Packing is a physical recovery action; it does not remotely transfer a worker or empty distant inventories.
