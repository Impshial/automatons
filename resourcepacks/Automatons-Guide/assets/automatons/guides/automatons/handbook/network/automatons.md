# Jobs, names and cargo

Right-click an owned Automaton to inspect its current task, tools and cargo. Select a profession from the list, then press **Apply Job**.
The first job applies directly. Changing an existing job asks for confirmation because it interrupts the worker and resets its current workload.
Shared buildings and actual items remain at their sites.

<ItemImage id="automatons:automaton" scale="3" />

Use the [Wand](wand.md) to assign the worker to a matching Totem. A profession alone is not an assignment.
Workers have **three retained tool slots** and normally **nine cargo slots**. Runningtons have eighteen cargo slots from their backpack.
Defaults can be changed by world settings. Unload excess courier cargo before changing away from Runnington.

Workers receive unique names. Their name-label toggle changes display, not identity. Find is available from the Totem's worker page.
Start/stop controls and home recall can pause an otherwise ready worker.

Packing preserves identity, name, home, job and assignment while dropping the actual tools and cargo once. Pick those items up before leaving.
An unloaded worker cannot act, transfer items, or be found by glow until its area is loaded.

All Automatons collect visible dropped items within one block of their body, even while paused, idle or waiting. This nearby cleanup does not send them away from their work. Items must have finished their pickup delay and be unclaimed or owned by you. They only take what fits; the remainder stays on the ground. Useful tools fill free profession tool slots first, with spare tools and other items stored in cargo.
