# Pieces of Coal and Charcoal

Split one Coal or Charcoal into **eight matching pieces**. Combine exactly eight of the same piece to recover its parent. Coal and charcoal pieces cannot be mixed for recombination.

Each piece has **one eighth of its parent's fuel time**: normally 200 furnace ticks, enough for one ordinary smelt. Blast furnaces and smokers use their normal faster timing and also process one item per piece.
Pieces use the familiar vanilla fuel textures, so read the item names when sorting. They do not count as full coal for torch or coal-block crafting.
