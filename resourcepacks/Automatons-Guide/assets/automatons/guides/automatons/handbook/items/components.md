# Automaton body parts

Craft **one head, one torso, two arms and two legs**, then assemble them at your home Pedestal.
The torso contains Lumina Cores; it is not itself an Empty Core or Lumina Core.

## Armored body

Guardington uses one armored head, one armored torso, two armored arms and two armored legs. Assemble a complete matching set; ordinary and armored components cannot be mixed. The armored torso still uses two Lumina Cores. Iron replaces the ordinary parts' log ingredients.
