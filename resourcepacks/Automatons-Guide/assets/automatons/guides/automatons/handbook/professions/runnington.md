# Runnington

Assign a Runnington to a completed **Warehouse**. Its backpack doubles default cargo to **18 slots**, with three separate tool slots.
It physically carries permitted supplies to workers and collects surplus output from registered worksite chests, including mine depots.

Runningtons **never withdraw tools, Hammers, torches or saplings from chests**. Supply these locally for the worker that needs them.
Farm planting reserves stay behind too, including carrots and potatoes needed for replanting. Warehouse stock is not collected into another warehouse in a loop.

Open **Totem → Logistics** to read each worker's task, requests and deliveries. A request is not a remote transfer: the runner must reach the source and recipient.
Deliveries reserve real stock and destination capacity. Unloaded areas wait; blocked routes need repair.

Canceled parcels return to warehouse storage. If storage is full, the runner keeps its cargo and retries. Recall also preserves parcel ownership.
Before changing away from Runnington, unload until its items fit the normal cargo capacity.
