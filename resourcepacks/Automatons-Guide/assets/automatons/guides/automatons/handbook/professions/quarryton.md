# Quarryton

Excavates the supplied 15×15 footprint in complete horizontal layers. The default bottom is the dimension's minimum build height; Settings may choose a higher bottom. Bedrock stays intact. Quarrying is separate from Minington strip mining.

The access ladder and its backing remain outside the excavation footprint. Supply real ladders, suitable filler and torches, and a usable pickaxe. A Quarryton waits for a pickaxe before beginning excavation and requests a replacement when it breaks. Shovels are optional for soil; they do not replace the required pickaxe. Harder blocks require the appropriate pickaxe tier. Workers contain reachable fluids with paid filler and keep workspaces at block-light level eight. Caves pause work for inspection and acknowledgement. A full inventory returns through the surface route to unload; waiting for a path, tool or storage never marks cells finished.
