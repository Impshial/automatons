# Runnerton
Assign an ordinary worker to a Warehouse Totem. Its profession changes automatically and its backpack has eighteen slots. It serves sites on the same owned home network.

Explicit requests support tools, rods, weapons, arrows, torches, filler, breeding food, shears, buckets, leads, flowers, planting stock, bottles, Luminall containers, fuel, shards and construction materials. Bulk output collection preserves tools, armor, Hammers, torches, saplings, field planting reserves and working supplies. Equipped items and display armor are not loose output.

Every extraction/insertion occurs at a real reachable loaded inventory. Ownership, current demand, storage access and reservations are rechecked. Underground depots and the mage tower use physical access routes. Full destinations preserve cargo; cancelled requests return or reroute real items.

Reassignment to a smaller inventory consolidates cargo and incompatible equipment into cargo and available linked chests. If all items cannot fit, the change is rejected without loss. Recall suspends deliveries while preserving reservations.

A Crafterton stays in the workshop while a deployed same-network Runnerton exists. Without one, it can fetch ingredients and deliver completed products itself.
