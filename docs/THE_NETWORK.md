# The Network
A home Automaton Pedestal defines an owned network. Totems, workers and Machines retain identities across saves and packing. Replacing a block at the same coordinates does not inherit its identity.

## Assignment and menus
Configure a Totem before assignment. Its chooser contains eleven production purposes. Assignment automatically selects the profession; worker menus have no job selector. Configured sites accept workers before construction finishes. Buildingtons can build their own first shed; other production waits for a ready site.

A Totem accepts six ordinary production workers. Reassignment preserves identity, ownership, health, energy and real inventory. Incompatible equipment moves to cargo or available linked chests; insufficient space rejects the change without partial mutation. Guards require armored bodies, cannot change profession, and use a separate roster. Direct Machine assignment selects Operatorton.

Totems have Overview, Workers and Settings tabs. Overview contains construction progress and Build List. Workers lists production workers and guards. Settings contains purpose-specific controls. Changing purpose requires confirmation and pauses replacement construction.

Worker menus have Status and Inventory tabs with read-only profession, health, operating energy, activity, equipment and cargo. Luminaritons also show service reserve; guards have armor slots and patrol controls.

Call All Home persists until released and takes precedence over work. It preserves assignments/progress and temporarily moves guards to the ten-block home perimeter. Release restores their previous patrol mode.

## Construction and storage
Buildingtons use actual blueprint materials, including workstations and display contents. Containers start empty. Omitted and structure-void cells preserve terrain; explicit air clears permitted obstructions. Valuable block entities, protected blocks and unbreakable blocks stop conflicting work.

Partial supplies are sufficient to begin. Missing resources remain visible in Build List. Temporary construction chests hold actual items and are removed only when safely emptied. Building logs are protected from forestry.

Recognized local chests supply and store by default. Both halves of double chests must be loaded and usable. Couriers move actual stock between owned sites, with reservations and final transfer checks. Unloaded inventory is unavailable, never assumed empty.

## Production and recovery
Farm sites adopt suitable irregular farmland or prepare a shared 9×9 plot with 80 crop cells. Three ghost selectors control its strips; blank disables a strip. A new field needs actual planting stock, water and glass.

Mine sites build fixed access and four double chests at each landing; Quarry sites use a separate 15×15 plan. See [mining](MININGTON.md). All thirteen jobs have in-game profession pages.

Loaded time consumes operating energy. Low charge triggers bottle service; zero charge stops movement and work. Bottles do not heal. Threat retreat uses owned shelter and preserves work for resumption. Night alone does not stop work.
