# Concrete limits and extension points — 0.2.0

All eight specified Overworld tree families are enabled. Dedicated-server tests now grow oak, birch, spruce, jungle, dark oak, acacia, mangrove and cherry, then require a real worker to approach and commit a fall. This covers representative generated forms, not every seed, terrain arrangement or mod interaction.

- Species definitions bound height to 48 above the recognized root and branch spread to 12. Spruce/jungle allow 384 logs; the others allow 192. Configuration parser limits are separate safety ceilings. Owned crown/attachment transfer is bounded at 4096 blocks; natural attachment discovery is bounded at 512. Connected independent trunks, ambiguous construction, unloaded portions and genuinely unsafe conversions remain conservative rejections.
- Natural mangrove roots, their attached moss/propagules and natural jungle vines are included in bounded tree ownership. Placed attachments are tracked going forward and revalidated before conversion. Fluids in removed waterlogged attachments are preserved. Dense or flooded terrain still requires a reachable work position and safe grounded timber space.
- Direct placements are tracked after installation. Old untracked construction can be structurally ambiguous. A claimed natural tree is not permission to dismantle an arbitrary building or forest through touching crowns. Shared foliage is retained; unsupported retained leaves can decay through vanilla behavior.
- Forge break events use an owner-identified fake player for destructive permission checks. Specific third-party claim plugins and every custom handler are not certified. This protection feature does not replace a server-wide claim system or prevent ordinary player actions.
- Vanilla single/double chests work. Other chest-like storage must opt into `automatons:storage_chests` and expose an item handler. Arbitrary nearby machines are excluded. A double chest requires both halves loaded and in range.
- Compatible axes extend `AxeItem` or opt into `automatons:axes`. Workers expose their filtered GUI and bounded pickup/restocking paths, not an unrestricted automation inventory. Job switching keeps stored tools but disables them for Unassigned workers.
- Workers do not force-load chunks, teleport across dimensions, break routes through walls or move anchors with packed items. Unloaded links are preserved as unverified. A missing local home needs explicit rebind. High, isolated or blocked work can need terrain access supplied by the player.
- Each fall has one active worker processor. Packing and recall release it; an unavailable claimant expires after 200 loaded timber ticks. Players can process the same authoritative timber ledger. Uncommitted standing claims are rebuilt after load to avoid stale permanent locks.
- Ordinary saves persist identities, point balances, partial cores, inventories, recall, pending loot and consumed sections. Source transfer and item transactions occur on the server thread. Separate world-file writes do not provide an operating-system-crash transaction guarantee; maintain normal backups.
- Infusion saves exact charged points. An unfinished sub-point interval restarts after a reload or invalid donor state; no points are spent early and unloaded time adds no catch-up. Offline/dead/out-of-range donors cannot spend.
- There is no custom GUI for the infuser. Recipe-book entries cover ordinary crafting; the usage guide explains world infusion and six-slot assembly. JEI does not receive a separate custom infusion/assembly category in this version.
- The shared roster is paginated. A packed item that has been destroyed remains an administrative Packed entry until deliberately unbound. No arbitrary worker-count cap is imposed; throughput remains governed by server budgets and available space.

Editable extension points are the recipe JSON, block/item tags and `data/<namespace>/automatons_species/<name>.json`. Nether fungi, mushrooms and extra jobs are outside this release's requested scope. See `VERIFICATION.md` for executed checks and client/multiplayer coverage, rather than assuming every visual or modded scenario was tested.
