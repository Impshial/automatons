# Concrete limits and extension points — 0.6.0

- Mine construction and quarry excavation share a frontier. Tunnel miners claim separate saved branches with a common platform access passage. Tunnel mode remains at its initial chamber level. Quarry stairs consume retained perimeter space and stop on unsafe access, protected cells or unbreakable treads; eligible unbreakable center cells are recorded as exclusions.
- Mineheads are built by Buildingtons; Miningtons then descend 20 blocks from the open surface entrance. Four ordinary chests are required before new construction. Two double chests hold mined output underground; surface storage supplies tools and materials. Full/blocked underground storage waits for space; Runningtons can collect eligible output through the saved mine route.
- Fluid avoidance returns to the platform before trying an unused forward/side route and never deliberately opens the fluid boundary. With all three directions unavailable, the worker waits. Fluids in the shaft or unfinished platform still require intervention because no safe branching platform exists yet. Falling-block mining requires a reachable stance at least two blocks horizontally from the column, ordinary reach and line of sight; otherwise work pauses.
- Mining terrain and tool tags are explicit extension points. Claim integration uses standard owner-identified Forge break/place events; every third-party claim or inventory implementation is not certified. A safe placement/path can be unavailable on irregular terrain, requiring player repairs or a different Totem site. Mine infrastructure is preserved rather than silently recreated when a mode changes.
- Light coverage uses actual block light in managed standing spaces and bounded incremental rechecks. It does not light unrelated caves, repel existing mobs or promise protection from every special spawning rule. No chunks are loaded solely for mining, naming or label preferences.
- The server-wide name ledger reserves issued names permanently, including dead/lost workers and old names after a successful rename. It imports known saved worker records first. Previously unindexed legacy items are reconciled when carried or deployed; arbitrary third-party or unloaded inventories are not globally scanned. UUID/generation checks remain authoritative for duplicate packed items.
- The overhead-name toggle affects vanilla-style labels only. Menus and items retain names. Homes are identified by UUID; an unloaded or truly missing home retains the worker's cached setting. Labels use vanilla distance/visibility behavior and the Minecraft font, so available glyph shapes follow the active font/resource pack.

All eight specified Overworld tree families are enabled. Dedicated-server tests now grow oak, birch, spruce, jungle, dark oak, acacia, mangrove and cherry, then require a real worker to approach and commit a fall. This covers representative generated forms, not every seed, terrain arrangement or mod interaction.

- Species definitions bound height to 48 above the recognized root and branch spread to 12. Spruce/jungle allow 384 logs; the others allow 192. Configuration parser limits are separate safety ceilings. Owned crown/attachment transfer is bounded at 4096 blocks; natural attachment discovery is bounded at 512. Connected independent trunks or ambiguous construction can reject whole-tree conversion, then become individual log jobs inside the totem area. Unloaded portions and unsafe conversions remain deferred.
- Natural mangrove roots, their attached moss/propagules and natural jungle vines are included in bounded tree ownership. Placed attachments are tracked going forward and revalidated before conversion. Fluids in removed waterlogged attachments are preserved. Dense or flooded terrain still requires a reachable work position and safe connected timber space.
- Direct placements are tracked after installation. Old untracked construction can be structurally ambiguous. Lumberton assignment now authorizes harvesting placed/construction logs within the configured area. Whole-tree recognition and player felling retain natural-tree checks. Shared foliage outside a falling trunk’s swept corridor is retained; natural leaves inside the corridor can be trimmed. Unsupported retained leaves can decay through vanilla behavior.
- Forge break events use an owner-identified fake player for destructive permission checks. Specific third-party claim plugins and every custom handler are not certified. This protection feature does not replace a server-wide claim system or prevent ordinary player actions.
- Vanilla single/double chests work. Other chest-like storage must opt into `automatons:storage_chests` and expose an item handler. Arbitrary nearby machines are excluded. A double chest requires both halves loaded and in range, except explicitly registered underground depot containers belonging to that Totem. Warehouse chests use the same surface range as other local chests.
- Compatible axes extend `AxeItem` or opt into `automatons:axes`. Workers expose their filtered GUI, a live item-handler capability and bounded pickup/restocking paths. Job switching keeps stored tools but disables them for Unassigned workers.
- Workers do not force-load chunks, teleport across dimensions, break routes through walls or move anchors with packed items. Unloaded links are preserved as unverified. An unavailable home retains its identity; there is no Rebind button in the worker GUI. High, isolated or blocked work can need terrain access supplied by the player.
- Each fall has one active worker processor. Packing and recall release it; an unavailable claimant expires after 200 loaded timber ticks. Players can mine the real fallen blocks normally; the job records those removals without extra loot. Uncommitted standing claims are rebuilt after load to avoid stale permanent locks.
- Ordinary saves persist identities, point balances, partial shards and recoverable legacy cores, inventories, recall, pending loot and consumed sections. Source transfer and item transactions occur on the server thread. Separate world-file writes do not provide an operating-system-crash transaction guarantee; maintain normal backups.
- Infusion saves exact paid thirds and its active timer. Invalid donors reset the interval; energy is never spent early and unloaded time grants no catch-up. Offline/dead/out-of-range donors cannot spend.
- The infuser operates in the world rather than a container GUI. Optional JEI integration shows both world infusion and six-slot assembly, including inputs, outputs and their pedestal catalysts. Assembly recipes come from synchronized server data. JEI does not automatically transfer components or spend Lumina.
- The shared roster is paginated. A packed item that has been destroyed remains an administrative Packed entry until deliberately unbound. No arbitrary worker-count cap is imposed; throughput remains governed by server budgets and available space.

Editable extension points are the recipe JSON, block/item tags and `data/<namespace>/automatons_species/<name>.json`. Whole-fungus and mushroom recognition and extra jobs are outside this release’s requested scope; individual blocks/items in the vanilla logs tags can still be harvested/collected within range. See `VERIFICATION.md` for executed checks and client/multiplayer coverage, rather than assuming every visual or modded scenario was tested.

The area preview is client-local and renders one topmost supported air cell per horizontal column within the configured vertical band. It follows hills and roofs rather than drawing a solid volume or forcing chunks to load. It marks configured coverage; an inaccessible log still needs a reachable route.

Dropped-log collection requires ground contact, available cargo space, visibility and a reachable path inside the Totem’s logging/height bounds. Unloaded items are not sought. The new small Lumina values are stored as exact thirds; core recipes and infusion costs remain whole points.

All falls materialize as ordinary sideways log blocks on landing. Existing virtual worker timber converts when its target cells are available. A hidden job record tracks processing and pending cargo; periodic sweeps remove obsolete records and client animations without deleting world blocks. New falls preserve trunk/branch geometry and try four directions, with at most eight blocks of vertical clearance adjustment. The model rotates as a single connected object; it does not simulate bending, bouncing, or fracture physics. A grounded contact can support sections bridging a depression. The root selects the work area; the full-length fall may extend past the Totem boundary, while loaded-chunk and protection checks still apply.

## 0.4.0 logistics boundaries

No automatic cross-owner, cross-home or cross-dimensional transport; no forced chunk loading. Construction gathers its complete remaining kit into a temporary outside chest before building, and requires reachable storage, suitable exterior footing and accepted permission hooks. A Buildington claims one project until completion or an explicit lifecycle release; Runningtons transport its eligible supplies. The queue handles one item type per parcel, with bounded collection batches and fairness, rather than global route optimization. Unloaded routes defer; damaged shafts or a changed building may need player repair. Finished warehouse blocks are rebuilt only through explicit Repair. Temporary chest cleanup waits if home storage is full, its lid is blocked, or it has been joined into a double chest; it never discards contents. Vanilla usable double chests are the supported warehouse containers. Handler simulation follows Forge's normal contract; misbehaving third-party inventory implementations are not certified. Full storage retains cargo. Miner lamps are visual. Multiple human clients and arbitrary protection mods require additional integration testing.

## 0.4.1 construction and planting

Build lists count loaded, recognized inventory; they do not promise a route to every displayed item or count unregistered distant storage. Only blueprint cells and actual supplies govern continued building; missing stock is a recoverable wait. Automatic clearing removes breakable obstructions in required structural cells, subject to protection hooks; unbreakable blocks require a different site or player intervention. Extra interior workstations/containers in blueprint air cells are preserved. A project keeps its saved purpose, blueprint version and orientation.

A paused miner holds its current place, but fluid hazards, full depots, missing tools, denied placement or an unsafe route can still stop work. Mining never bypasses these conditions to meet a continuous-work request.

Replanting needs a carried sapling and valid replaceable, dry planting space within the forestry range. It uses placement hooks and normal sapling survival rules. Dark oak requires four saplings and a clear 2×2 area. Other saplings are planted singly. When cargo has no suitable sapling, Lumbertons can walk to recognized local storage and replenish up to sixteen of one suitable type. Replanting does not use bone meal, force growth, or replace blocked ground.

## 0.4.2 access and supplies

Temporary forestry pillars use real dirt, cobblestone or planks carried by the worker. They need a clear, supported vertical column, enough material, loaded chunks and placement permission. Workers remove their journaled supports top-down and recover the drops; changed blocks or occupied cleanup cells stop removal safely. The default vertical search band is 32 blocks above/below the Totem.

Runningtons leave all tools, ordinary/soul/redstone torches and saplings in chest inventories, including staging chests. Supply construction torches and replacement tools locally for the Buildington to collect. Each tunnel worker keeps a separate saved branch; the quarry still shares one active excavation face.

## 0.5.0 worksites and farming

Each Totem uses one purpose-specific horizontal range for work and nearby chests. There is no inner exclusion or separate storage radius. Underground mine depots and excavation use their saved infrastructure. A whole farm plot must fit inside its Totem range; an existing irregular field is adopted only for eligible cells. Expanded or changed terrain may require another bounded discovery pass.

Farmington supports wheat, carrots, potatoes and beetroot. It reserves one shared level 9×9 plot when no existing field is found in a fully loaded survey. It does not terraform hills, supply free water, grow trees, farm animals or harvest melons/pumpkins. Bone meal is optional and uses ordinary crop growth. Workers and parcels preserve planting reserves, but blocked paths, unavailable tools, protected land and unloaded chunks can still pause individual tasks.

Construction verifies blueprint cells and checks vanilla survival/attachment rules, reach, collision and permission before placing. Ordinary solid blocks follow vanilla survival rules; the builder does not simulate structural engineering or temporary scaffolding. Claims survive short shortages and worker unloading. Changed hard blocks and unrelated containers require intervention. Cosmetic damage to a ready site does not reset its production; unusable chest lids or mine access still block the affected operation.

The first shed and a shared two-farmer field are exercised through actual worker AI. Rotated-template checks place fixtures directly to verify states, usable double chests and item-frame support; they are not twenty complete construction playthroughs. No multiple-human-client or arbitrary third-party protection/inventory compatibility certification is claimed.

## Lumina and existing saves

Automatons-owned commands and retrogen execution are removed in 0.7.0. Old history is retained as inert data; no job, ticket or terrain rewrite resumes. Workers still never force-load chunks. Legacy paid credit with no recorded donor remains preserved rather than assigning that energy to an invented owner. See LUMINA.md.

GuideME 20.1.15 is required on client and server. Its pinned implementation exposes only the fixed light theme. Optional JER displays a labeled estimate of bundled default candidate placements, not surveyed ore density or remote-server data-pack overrides. The handbook explains this distinction. Construction uses a reachable supported position outside the footprint; fully enclosed sites still require a route and safe outside footing. Supply transfers still require physical reach.

Pickaxe-only forestry obstructions need an active Minington on the same Network with a capable pickaxe and free cargo space. Helpers do not force-load chunks or bypass protected blocks, containers, fluid hazards or unreachable access. Without a suitable miner the request stays visible and the Lumberton tries other trees. Requests expire after five minutes without renewal; tree reconsideration can renew them. A request never authorizes breaking a replacement block.
