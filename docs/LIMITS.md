# Concrete limits and extension points — 0.2.4

All eight specified Overworld tree families are enabled. Dedicated-server tests now grow oak, birch, spruce, jungle, dark oak, acacia, mangrove and cherry, then require a real worker to approach and commit a fall. This covers representative generated forms, not every seed, terrain arrangement or mod interaction.

- Species definitions bound height to 48 above the recognized root and branch spread to 12. Spruce/jungle allow 384 logs; the others allow 192. Configuration parser limits are separate safety ceilings. Owned crown/attachment transfer is bounded at 4096 blocks; natural attachment discovery is bounded at 512. Connected independent trunks or ambiguous construction can reject whole-tree conversion, then become individual log jobs inside the totem area. Unloaded portions and unsafe conversions remain deferred.
- Natural mangrove roots, their attached moss/propagules and natural jungle vines are included in bounded tree ownership. Placed attachments are tracked going forward and revalidated before conversion. Fluids in removed waterlogged attachments are preserved. Dense or flooded terrain still requires a reachable work position and safe connected timber space.
- Direct placements are tracked after installation. Old untracked construction can be structurally ambiguous. Lumberton assignment now authorizes harvesting placed/construction logs within the configured area. Whole-tree recognition and player felling retain natural-tree checks. Shared foliage outside a falling trunk’s swept corridor is retained; natural leaves inside the corridor can be trimmed. Unsupported retained leaves can decay through vanilla behavior.
- Forge break events use an owner-identified fake player for destructive permission checks. Specific third-party claim plugins and every custom handler are not certified. This protection feature does not replace a server-wide claim system or prevent ordinary player actions.
- Vanilla single/double chests work. Other chest-like storage must opt into `automatons:storage_chests` and expose an item handler. Arbitrary nearby machines are excluded. A double chest requires both halves loaded and in range.
- Compatible axes extend `AxeItem` or opt into `automatons:axes`. Workers expose their filtered GUI and bounded pickup/restocking paths, not an unrestricted automation inventory. Job switching keeps stored tools but disables them for Unassigned workers.
- Workers do not force-load chunks, teleport across dimensions, break routes through walls or move anchors with packed items. Unloaded links are preserved as unverified. A missing local home needs explicit rebind. High, isolated or blocked work can need terrain access supplied by the player.
- Each fall has one active worker processor. Packing and recall release it; an unavailable claimant expires after 200 loaded timber ticks. Players can process the same authoritative timber ledger. Uncommitted standing claims are rebuilt after load to avoid stale permanent locks.
- Ordinary saves persist identities, point balances, partial cores, inventories, recall, pending loot and consumed sections. Source transfer and item transactions occur on the server thread. Separate world-file writes do not provide an operating-system-crash transaction guarantee; maintain normal backups.
- Infusion saves exact charged points. An unfinished sub-point interval restarts after a reload or invalid donor state; no points are spent early and unloaded time adds no catch-up. Offline/dead/out-of-range donors cannot spend.
- The infuser operates in the world rather than a container GUI. Optional JEI integration shows both world infusion and six-slot assembly, including inputs, outputs and their pedestal catalysts. Assembly recipes come from synchronized server data. JEI does not automatically transfer components or spend Magica.
- The shared roster is paginated. A packed item that has been destroyed remains an administrative Packed entry until deliberately unbound. No arbitrary worker-count cap is imposed; throughput remains governed by server budgets and available space.

Editable extension points are the recipe JSON, block/item tags and `data/<namespace>/automatons_species/<name>.json`. Whole-fungus and mushroom recognition and extra jobs are outside this release’s requested scope; individual blocks/items in the vanilla logs tags can still be harvested/collected within range. See `VERIFICATION.md` for executed checks and client/multiplayer coverage, rather than assuming every visual or modded scenario was tested.

The area preview is client-local and renders one topmost supported air cell per horizontal column within the configured vertical band. It follows hills and roofs rather than drawing a solid volume or forcing chunks to load. It marks configured coverage; an inaccessible log still needs a reachable route.

Dropped-log collection requires ground contact, available cargo space, visibility and a reachable path inside the Totem’s logging/height bounds. Unloaded items are not sought. The new small Magica values are stored as exact thirds; core recipes and infusion costs remain whole points.

Player-triggered falls created from 0.2.3 onward materialize as ordinary block states once their complete landing is safe. Existing 0.2.2 timber lacks a reliable player-origin marker and retains its existing processing behavior. New falls preserve trunk/branch geometry and try four directions, with at most eight blocks of vertical clearance adjustment. The model rotates as a single connected object; it does not simulate bending, bouncing, or fracture physics. A grounded contact can support sections bridging a depression. The root selects the work area; the full-length fall may extend past the Totem boundary, while loaded-chunk and protection checks still apply.
